## What it is

A course platform where instructors publish lessons and students study them with AI help.

## How it works

A Laravel 10 backend sits on a MySQL schema we built up over 17 migrations. Session auth and role middleware keep student and instructor routes apart.

An AI model writes quizzes from each lesson, and a chatbot answers students' questions from the lesson they have open.
